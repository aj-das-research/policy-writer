PROJECT A — Who guards the policy writer?

Papers: 
Shi et al., Progent, 2025; Debenedetti et al., AgentDojo.

Claim: 
An LLM turns the user's query into a deterministic policy over tool calls: with GPT-4o, ASR falls from 39.9% to 1.0% at similar utility.

Gap: 
The policy writer is assumed reliable and not itself injectable. In the released AgentDojo integration, policy updates are computed from tool outputs and applied without confirmation; the narrowing-only check is off by default.

Implement: 
With Progent's code (suite v1.2), check each initial policy against the ground-truth calls of the user task and of every injection task; then replay the shipped traces' tool outputs through the update step.

Measure: 
False blocks, attacks blocked, and attacker values entering the policy after updates.

Ablate: 
Policy model; narrowing-only updates on or off.

Control: 
Allow-all and deny-all policies.
